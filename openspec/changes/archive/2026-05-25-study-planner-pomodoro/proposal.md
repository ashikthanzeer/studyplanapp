## Why

Students struggle to manage their study time effectively and stay focused on multiple subjects and assignments. A dedicated Study Planner with integrated Pomodoro timer helps students break down study sessions into manageable chunks, track progress visually, and maintain better focus and productivity across their coursework.

## What Changes

- New web application dedicated to student study planning and time management
- Integration of Pomodoro technique (25-min focus sessions with breaks) as core feature
- Task management system for assignments, study goals, and exams
- Visual task organization with Kanban board view for workload management
- Study session tracking and analytics
- Notification system for Pomodoro reminders and upcoming deadlines
- Student-friendly UI optimized for desktop and tablet use

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `pomodoro-timer`: Configurable Pomodoro timer with focus/break sessions, notifications, and session tracking
- `task-management`: Create, update, organize, and prioritize study tasks and assignments with due dates and descriptions
- `kanban-board`: Visual task organization using Kanban board with customizable columns (e.g., To Do, In Progress, Done)
- `time-tracking`: Automatic time tracking for study sessions, with session history and time analytics
- `notifications`: Desktop and in-app notifications for Pomodoro intervals, break reminders, and upcoming deadlines
- `student-profiles`: Student profile with study preferences, subject list, and study goals
- `dashboard`: Overview of today's tasks, active Pomodoro session, and productivity stats
